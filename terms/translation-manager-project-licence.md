## Data and Telemetry

Jumoo receives no Customer Data. Jumoo has no access to your content, your configuration, your credentials, or any personal data held within your Umbraco installation.

The Software makes an outbound licence validation request to Jumoo. That request transmits the licence key and nothing further. No domain name, no site content, no configuration, no usage statistics and no personal data are transmitted to Jumoo at any point. Jumoo uses the request solely to verify the validity of the licence key.

The Software does not depend on that request in order to function. If the request cannot be completed, for any reason including the absence of outbound network access, the Software will continue to operate normally. This applies both to initial activation of a licence key and to ongoing use, so no instance within the Pipeline requires outbound connectivity to Jumoo at any stage.

The Software is designed to send content to Translation Providers for translation. Where you configure a Translation Provider, the Software will transmit the content you submit for translation, together with the associated language and formatting information required to perform that translation, to that Translation Provider using the credentials you supply. That transmission is made directly from your Umbraco installation to the Translation Provider and does not pass through Jumoo.

You choose which Translation Providers to configure, and no content is sent to any Translation Provider unless you have configured one. Your use of a Translation Provider is governed by your own agreement with that Translation Provider. You are responsible for the terms on which you use it, and for ensuring that you have the right to send the content you submit, including any personal data it contains, to that Translation Provider.

Jumoo does not act as a data processor or data controller in respect of Customer Data under this agreement.
